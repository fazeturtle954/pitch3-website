Common Questions

Before you start.

How old does my son need to be?

We work with pitchers from age 10 through pro. The program is built around what's appropriate for your son's age and stage of development — we assign the right starting point after watching the intake video.

What if he's already on a throwing program?

Tell us about it in the intake form. We'll either build around what you're doing or recommend transitioning — whichever serves your son better.

When does his program start?

Within 24 hours of you signing up. Your son's coach personally reviews the intake video, builds the first program, and it's in his hands the next morning through the coaching app.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Yes, anytime — self-service through your Stripe account. No phone calls, no retention pitches. Cancel and you won't be charged again.

What if he gets injured?

Email us right away. We'll pause the program at no cost while he heals, then build a return-to-throwing plan when he's ready. Your subscription pauses too — you don't pay for time you're not training.

Do you offer in-person training too?

Yes — see our In-Person page for Fort Lauderdale-based small-group training.
